Review Wallet, a backend-free example wallet app, in English with the `explore` build profile. Everything runs on the device: accounts, balances, transfers, top-ups and purchases are local, and every success, failure and loading state follows fixed rules. No server, payment provider, real account or secret is involved. The profile fixes the clock at 1 September 2026 10:00 UTC, renders final animation frames and shows loading indicators as static dots.

Some routes may exist on only one of the two revisions, for example a new auth screen or a removed onboarding step. Follow the steps for the routes the current revision has, skip the steps for routes it lacks, and never invent a route or control. Use one representative capture per source-selected target. Follow the order below on both revisions so each screen is captured in the same state. Each side starts from a fresh app sandbox with no account.

Choose capture landmarks from visible, stable labels such as screen titles and button labels that exist on both revisions. Dismiss keyboards and let navigation settle before capturing. If iOS asks to save a password, choose Not Now. Opening a link from outside the app may show iOS's "Open in Wallet?" prompt; choose Open. Toasts disappear after about two seconds; wait for them before capturing.

Do not grant permissions or change appearance. No step needs a permission, and a fresh install has none. Nearby ATMs, Scan to pay, Your contacts, Notifications and Privacy & permissions ask iOS only when a button is pressed, so capture them in the state they open in and never press Use my location, Scan a code, From contacts, Allow access, Allow, Open Settings or the Transaction alerts switch: an answered prompt changes those screens for the rest of the run, and only iPhone Settings can undo it. Leave Settings → Appearance on System, and leave the iPhone's own appearance, text size and accessibility settings as they are; every screen follows them. Also do not change the currency, add cards, freeze cards, set a card limit, edit the display name or search Activity; those change later screens.
